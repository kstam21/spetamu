What We Do

Your gateway to the world of polymers and plastics

The Society of Plastics Engineers (SPE) is a global community connecting passionate students and professionals through cutting-edge education, groundbreaking research, and networking opportunities — and our TAMU chapter brings that community to campus.

Connect

We link students with practicing plastics and polymer engineers through networking events, industry talks, and mentorship.

Learn

Technical talks, workshops, and industry exposure bridge classroom learning with real-world plastics engineering practice.

Grow

We build leadership, communication, and career-ready skills through chapter involvement and SPE's national resources.
